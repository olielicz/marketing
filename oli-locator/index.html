Questions, answered honestly

Where do the leads come from?

Three real, live data sources: Adzuna (jobs API covering USA, UK, and Australia — 250+ API calls/day), Remotive (global remote jobs, no API key required), and Jobicy (global remote tech jobs, no key required). All three are free, public APIs — no scraping, no grey-area data collection.

What skill categories are covered?

11 categories: Web Development, Mobile App Dev, UI/UX Design, Digital Marketing, Content Writing, Video/Animation, Virtual Assistant, Data Entry/Admin, Accounting/Bookkeeping, Sales/BDR, and Customer Support. More categories are planned — let us know if you'd like to see yours added.

What countries are covered?

USA 🇺🇸, UK 🇬🇧, and Australia 🇦🇺 via Adzuna (location-based leads with city/postcode data). Plus Global Remote 🌍 via Remotive and Jobicy — work-from-anywhere opportunities regardless of your location.

How is this different from Upwork or Fiverr?

Oli-Locator finds leads before they post on freelance marketplaces. Companies posting on job boards need freelancers now — you reach them directly instead of competing with 50+ proposals on a marketplace. No platform fees, no race to the bottom on pricing, no middleman taking 20%.

Do I need to cold-pitch?

Yes — but these are warm leads. Every lead is a company that has actively posted looking for the exact skill you offer. You're not blasting strangers; you're reaching out to someone who already raised their hand and said "I need this done." That's a much higher conversion rate than cold outreach to random businesses.

What's the real difference between Starter, Pro, and Agency?

Real, server-enforced limits: Starter ($49/mo or $468/yr) — all 3 tabs (Client Leads, Gov Contracts, Funded Startups), all countries + global remote, up to 200 searches/day (resets at midnight UTC), save leads, inbox + call log, 1 seat only. Pro ($129/mo or $1,188/yr) — everything in Starter plus unlimited searches (no daily cap), priority cache. Agency ($299/mo or $2,748/yr) — everything in Pro plus up to 10 team seats (real separate logins, shared data) and API access (Bearer token for programmatic use). Requesting more searches on Starter, or adding team members on Pro, is genuinely refused with a clear upgrade message.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Checkout runs through Stripe and PayPal — the two most widely used payment processors in the world. Your card is billed monthly (or annually at a discount) and you can cancel anytime from your account settings.
