Questions, answered honestly

Which countries and trades does this cover?

Oli-Locator covers United States 🇺🇸, United Kingdom 🇬🇧, and Australia 🇦🇺 — search by city, suburb, postcode, or region within any of the three countries. Leads span 11 trades: cleaning, pest control, renovation, roofing, painting, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, HVAC, flooring, and handyman. More markets and trades are planned — contact us if you'd like to see yours added.

Where do the leads actually come from?

Three real, compliant sources: (1) your own homeowner-facing "Request a Quote" page — first-party, consented leads, the cleanest source there is; (2) live public job/demand feeds where terms of use permit it; (3) demo data for trying the app before you connect real sources. We do not scrape personal data from social media or directories — that's a real legal risk under GDPR/CCPA, not just bad practice, and we built this to avoid it entirely.

Can I use my own phone/calling app?

Yes — that's by design. Tapping a number in Oli-Locator opens your device's default dialer via a standard tap-to-call link, so whatever calling app you already use (your carrier's dialer, a VoIP app, a dedicated business-line app) just works. No forced platform, no bundled calling subscription.

Is there a device limit?

No — Oli-Locator is a hosted, login-based app (not something you download or self-host), so log in from as many devices as you personally use day to day. There's no separate serial code or device cap for this product.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Checkout runs through Stripe and PayPal — the two most widely used payment processors in the world. Your card is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime from your account settings.
