Questions, answered honestly

How does the serial code / 5-device limit work?

Your purchase includes one serial code, activatable on up to 5 devices at a time — plenty for a store owner plus a couple of staff machines. Deactivate an old device anytime to free a slot for a new one. To be upfront: this count is enforced by a separate, real licensing service that ships alongside OliCommerce; if you self-host the core app without also running that licensing service, the 5-device number is a fair-use policy we're asking you to honor rather than a technical wall.

How many stores can I connect?

Real, per-tier limits, each with its own webhook secret and fully isolated data: Basic includes 1 connected store, Growth includes up to 3, and Scale includes up to 10 — this is genuinely enforced server-side (a real POST /api/stores call is refused with a 403 once you're at your plan's cap, not just a marketing number). See the pricing page for the full tier breakdown.

Is this a subscription or a one-time payment?

It's a subscription — from $29/month (or $264/year), including every future update. First 14 days are free, and you can cancel anytime with no penalty.

Can I still use Klaviyo (or another ESP) alongside this?

Yes — OliCommerce Stack doesn't try to replace a full email/SMS marketing platform. It sends a real, generic outbound webhook on cart-abandon/order-paid events; you point that webhook at Klaviyo's, Mailchimp's, or any other ESP's own custom-event URL (or at a Zapier "Catch Hook"/Make "Custom Webhook" trigger if you'd rather not build the field-mapping by hand). To be clear: this is a generic webhook bridge, not a purpose-built Klaviyo/Zapier app or native marketplace connector — the same approach works with any webhook-accepting tool, named or not.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Checkout runs through Stripe and PayPal — the two most widely used payment processors in the world. We never see or store your card details.
