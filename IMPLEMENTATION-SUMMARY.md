## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Ecosystem is a non-issue when framed correctly**
   - Instead of "limited integrations," say "9,000+ accessible at 1/3 cost"
   - Zapier bridge becomes a feature, not a workaround
   - Honest positioning builds credibility

2. **Pricing power is in bundling, not exclusivity**
   - Competitors charge separately; Oli bundles
   - Customers save 50-80% not by Oli being cheaper per feature, but because they don't buy features they don't need
   - Highlight the "no per-user scaling" advantage heavily

3. **Email-to-upgrade is a proven funnel**
   - 8-email sequence designed to educate, not push
   - Social proof + ROI numbers matter more than urgency
   - 30-day guarantee dramatically reduces objections

4. **Landing pages need honesty + ROI**
   - Show what you gain by upgrading
   - Show what you don't get (be honest about limitations)
   - Use real numbers (hours saved, revenue recovered, costs avoided)

5. **Upgrade revenue is quick wins + long tail**
   - Premium Support tier is easiest (already needed by many)
   - Multi-Store/Team tiers are natural scaling
   - White-Label is the unicorn revenue play (high margin, few customers)
