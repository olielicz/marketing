Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to OliOps Suite, OliCommerce Stack, OliFlow Automation Engine, OliExplore, Oli-Locator, and OliSalesTrack

🛡️ 14-day free trial + 30-day money-back guarantee on all products
Jump to: 1. 14-day free trial 2. 30-day money-back guarantee 3. Cancellation & billing 4. How to request a refund 5. How refunds are processed 6. Exceptions 7. Per-product details 8. Contact

1. 14-day free trial

Every Oli Tools subscription — across all 6 products — starts with a 14-day free trial. During this period:

  • You are not charged anything. Your payment method is recorded but no money is taken.
  • You get full access to the product's features at your chosen tier.
  • You can cancel at any time during the trial with zero cost — no charge, no questions.
  • If you don't cancel, your first paid billing cycle begins automatically on day 15.

Our philosophy: we'd rather you try the tool risk-free and decide it's not for you, than have you pay upfront and feel stuck. The trial is real — no feature limits, no watermarks, no "trial mode" restrictions.

2. 30-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied after your first paid charge goes through, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

  • This applies to your first paid billing cycle only (i.e. the first charge after your 14-day trial ends).
  • Email us within 30 days of that first charge with your order details, and we'll refund you in full.
  • No justification needed — "it's not for me" is a perfectly valid reason.

Combined with the 14-day free trial, this means you effectively have 44 days (14 trial + 30 guarantee) to decide whether a tool is right for you, completely risk-free.

3. Cancellation & billing

All Oli Tools subscriptions are month-to-month or annual — cancel anytime, no long-term contracts, no cancellation fees.

  • Monthly subscriptions: cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period you've already paid for. No further charges after cancellation.
  • Annual subscriptions: cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of the current annual period. No further charges after cancellation. No partial refund for the remaining months of a yearly plan (the discount you received accounts for this commitment), except under the 30-day guarantee above.
  • No refund for the current billing period already in progress — you've already paid for that period and you keep access through it.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund under the 30-day guarantee or to report an erroneous charge:

  1. Email us via the Contact page (select "Billing & refunds" as the topic) or email directly.
  2. Include your order email address and product name so we can locate your account quickly.
  3. We'll confirm receipt within 1–2 business days and process the refund.

If you believe you were charged in error (double charge, charge after cancellation, etc.), contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll investigate and refund immediately if confirmed.

6. Exceptions

Refunds are not available in the following situations:

  • After the 30-day guarantee window: once more than 30 days have passed since your first paid charge, the money-back guarantee no longer applies. You can still cancel to stop future charges.
  • Abuse or violation of Terms: if your account was terminated for violating our Terms of Service (e.g. redistribution of the software, fraudulent activity), no refund is owed.
  • Chargeback disputes filed simultaneously: if you file a chargeback/dispute with your bank while also requesting a refund from us, we'll work with the payment provider to resolve it — but please contact us first, as chargebacks incur fees that we can avoid if you reach out directly.
