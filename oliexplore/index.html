Questions, answered honestly

How does the serial code / 5-device limit work?

Your purchase includes one serial code, activatable on up to 5 devices — your laptop, a work machine, whatever you use day to day. Free up a slot anytime by deactivating an old device.

Do you ever see or store my social media passwords?

No. Connecting a real account opens that platform's own official login popup (Facebook's, X's, TikTok's, etc.) — OliExplore never sees your password, only a token that platform issues after you approve access, which you can revoke anytime from that platform's own settings.

Does it work without connecting real accounts?

Yes — OliExplore ships with a full demo mode that works completely offline with sample posts, so you can try the entire collect → recycle → publish flow before connecting anything real.

Are there any platform-specific limits?

Yes, and we'd rather tell you upfront: X currently requires a paid API tier on X's own side to post via any third-party app (a cost X charges, not us), and TikTok restricts unaudited apps to private-only video visibility until that app passes TikTok's own review. Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and LinkedIn have no such restriction for standard use.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Checkout runs through Stripe and PayPal — the two most widely used payment processors in the world. We never see or store your card details.
