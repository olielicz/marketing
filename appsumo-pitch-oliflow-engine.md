## Deal description

**The problem:**
Agencies and ops teams running enough automations to matter end up paying $20–70/month on Zapier or Make, forever, with costs that scale with task volume — not with the value delivered. None of that spend results in owning anything; stop paying and the automations stop running.

**The solution:**
OliFlow is a self-hosted, zero-dependency workflow automation engine with a visual builder:
- **Visual builder** — drag-and-drop automation, no code required
- **Webhook triggers** — connect any CRM/SaaS that can send a webhook
- **Headless REST API** — or run it entirely programmatically
- **Prebuilt template pack** (via the companion auto-tools library) — ready-to-import n8n workflows, Make blueprints, Zapier-style configs, and 7 CRM adapters

**Why AppSumo buyers specifically:**
- AppSumo's audience skews toward agencies and solo operators managing multiple client automations — the exact profile hit hardest by per-task pricing
- Self-hosted, flat-fee subscription → predictable cost regardless of usage is exactly what a per-task-metering-fatigued audience is shopping for
- The template pack lowers the "blank canvas" barrier that usually slows self-hosted tool adoption

**Differentiation:**
Most AppSumo automation-category deals are either (a) another metered SaaS with a discounted LTD price that still caps usage, or (b) a narrow single-purpose integration. OliFlow is the rare "actually unlimited runs, flat price" pitch.
