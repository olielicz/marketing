1. **Always offer annual discount**: 10–15% off annual billing (current model)

3. **Free 14-day trial**: already live, don't change
4. **Upgrade trials**: When customer approaches base tier limits, offer 7-day free trial of upgrade tier
5. **Bundle discounts**: "Buy 2 upgrades, get 15% off total" (after Phase 2 proves demand)
