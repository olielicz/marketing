## Overview: Email Sequence Strategy

**Goal:** Convert 10-15% of free-trial users to paid ($39-119/mo per tool) within 30 days of signup

**Sequence Length:** 8 emails over 30 days
**Trigger:** Automated on Day 1 of free trial
**Personalization:** Tool-specific value propositions + upgrade benefits

1. **Days 1-7:** Education + success stories (no upsell)
2. **Days 8-14:** Feature demos + objection handling
3. **Days 15-22:** Trial expiration urgency + upgrade offer
4. **Days 23-30:** Final push + guarantee mention

## Implementation Guide

### Platform Setup:
- **Email Service:** Use Klaviyo, ConvertKit, or Brevo (free tier available)
- **Trigger:** Automated on free-trial signup
- **Personalization:** Pull first name + tool name + (if applicable) trial data (e.g., "You recovered $1,500 in abandoned carts")
- **CTA Buttons:** Link to upgrade page (`/upgrade/[tool-name]/`) or pricing page

### A/B Testing Strategy:
- **Subject lines:** Test A vs B vs C on first 100 signups per tool
- **Send times:** Test 9 AM vs 2 PM vs 6 PM (by user timezone)
- **CTA text:** Test "Upgrade now" vs "Start Pro trial" vs "Unlock Pro features"

### Success Metrics:
- **Open rate target:** 35-45% (email subject line matters)
- **Click rate target:** 8-12% (CTA relevance matters)
- **Conversion rate target:** 10-15% (trial → paid)
- **Cost per acquisition:** Roughly $0 (automated email) + $100-200 (landing page creation)

### Personalization Fields:
```
Hi [First Name],
Your [Tool Name] trial expires in [X] days.
You've set up [Y] [automations/campaigns/contacts].
That's saved you approximately [Z hours/dollars].
```

### Do's and Don'ts:
- ✅ **Do:** Share specific ROI numbers (e.g., "$500 recovered in abandoned carts")
- ✅ **Do:** Mention the 30-day money-back guarantee (reduces risk perception)
- ✅ **Do:** Ask for objections directly ("What stopped you?")
- ❌ **Don't:** Lie about features (Zapier has more integrations, own it)
- ❌ **Don't:** Create artificial urgency (trials actually expire, scarcity is real)
- ❌ **Don't:** Spam re-engagement (max 1 per day, skip weekends for B2B tools)

## FAQ for Email Marketers

**Q: Should I mention price in every email?**
A: No. Email 1-3 focus on value (time savings, ROI, success stories). Introduce pricing in Email 4 (trial expiration). By then, customers are emotionally invested.

**Q: What if they don't open the emails?**
A: Test subject lines aggressively. OliOps/OliFlow/Oli-Locator skew B2B (prefer "one question" / "honest comparison" subjects). OliCommerce/OliExplore/OliSalesTrack skew B2C/D2C (prefer "save $X" / "your data inside" subjects).

**Q: Should I mention competitors by name?**
A: Yes, but honestly. "Follow Up Boss is the industry standard for large brokerages. For solo agents, we're 30% cheaper." This builds credibility.

**Q: When should I send the final re-engagement email (Day 24)?**
A: Only send to users who didn't upgrade. If they upgraded, send a "welcome to Pro" email instead with onboarding tips.

**Q: Can I reuse these sequences for other tools?**
A: Yes, but swap pricing + competitor names. The structure (welcome → social proof → feature demo → urgency → last chance → re-engagement) works for any SaaS.
