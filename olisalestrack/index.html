Questions answered

Does it replace accounting software?

No — OliSalesTrack is a real-time sales and refund sync, not an accounting suite. It works alongside QuickBooks, Xero, or spreadsheets for your actual bookkeeping.

Where is my data stored?

Sales and refund events are stored on the server you (or we) deploy the sync service to — that's what makes live, real-time sync possible in the first place. Your dashboard login is required to view them; nothing is public.

Can I cancel the monthly plan anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your PayPal account. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

What exactly does the dashboard show me?

Three real, live numbers: Total Sales, Total Refunds, and Net Revenue (sales minus refunds — no cost-of-goods, processor fees, or expenses are deducted), plus a flat list of every synced transaction. We don't yet offer correlation analysis, expense tracking, or AI insights — seeing that get built is on our roadmap, not shipped today.

Can I connect Shopify, Stripe, or PayPal directly?

Yes — live webhook sync is real and verified (no manual CSV export/import needed). Sales and refunds sync in automatically as they happen, straight from Stripe, PayPal, or Shopify's own webhook events, with real signature verification rejecting anything forged or unsigned. See it in the live dashboard once you're subscribed.
