Questions answered

Does it replace accounting software?

OliSalesTrack is a performance tracker and correlation analyzer — not a full accounting suite. It works alongside QuickBooks, Xero, or spreadsheets. Think of it as your real-time business health dashboard.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally in your browser by default — nothing goes to any server. Your financial data stays private. The Pro plan adds optional encrypted cloud backup.

Can I cancel the monthly plan anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your PayPal account. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

What does "correlation analysis" actually show?

It calculates the Pearson correlation coefficient (r) between your sales, refunds, and expenses over time. For example, if r=0.8 between sales and refunds, it means high-sales days strongly predict more refunds — a signal to investigate your product quality or fulfillment process.

Can I import data from Shopify, Stripe, or PayPal?

Yes — via CSV export from any of those platforms. The importer auto-detects Shopify order export column names. Full API integration is on the Pro roadmap.
