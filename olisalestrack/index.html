Questions answered

Does it replace accounting software?

No — OliSalesTrack is a real-time sales and refund sync, not an accounting suite. It works alongside QuickBooks, Xero, or spreadsheets for your actual bookkeeping.

Where is my data stored?

Sales and refund events are stored on the server you (or we) deploy the sync service to — that's what makes live, real-time sync possible in the first place. Your dashboard login is required to view them; nothing is public.

Can I cancel the monthly plan anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your PayPal account. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

What does "correlation analysis" actually show?

A real Pearson correlation coefficient (r) between your sales and refunds over time, computed live in the app from your actual synced or CSV-imported data — not a made-up number. A value near 0 means refunds don't track your sales volume; closer to 1 means they tend to rise and fall together. AI-generated "insights" narrating what that number means are not built — you get the real number and chart, not an AI summary of it.

What exactly does the live-sync dashboard show me?

Three real, live numbers: Total Sales, Total Refunds, and Net Revenue (sales minus refunds — no cost-of-goods or processor fees deducted), plus a flat list of every synced transaction. Open the full app for the deeper view: correlation analysis, expense tracking with category ROI, a monthly P&L table, CSV import, and dispute-portal shortcuts — it also pulls in the same live-synced events shown here.

Can I connect Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, or Amazon directly?

Yes — live sync is real and verified (no manual CSV export/import needed). Sales and refunds sync in automatically as they happen, straight from Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, and WooCommerce's own webhook events (with real signature verification rejecting anything forged or unsigned), and Amazon orders sync in on a short polling interval from Amazon's own Selling Partner API. See it in the live dashboard once you're subscribed.
