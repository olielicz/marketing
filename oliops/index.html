Questions, answered honestly

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you'd rather not self-host, our managed hosting tier ($19/mo, optional and separate from the subscription license) runs everything for you — zero setup required on your end.

Is this a subscription or a one-time payment?

It's a subscription — Starter plan is $39/month (or $348/year) for access to all four connected tools (CRM, invoicing, payroll & accounting reports, AI support), including every future update. First 14 days are free, and you can cancel anytime. Pro ($69/mo) and Agency ($119/mo) tiers add higher device limits — see the pricing page for details.

How does the serial code / 5-device limit work?

Your purchase includes one serial code, activatable on up to 5 devices at a time (more on Pro/Agency tiers). Setting up a new machine? Deactivate an old device from your account (self-service, no email needed) to free up a slot. This isn't hardware-locked — it's a simple, fair device count, not copy-protection. To be upfront: this device count is enforced by a separate, real licensing service that ships alongside OliOps; if you self-host the core app without also running that licensing service, the 5-device number is a fair-use policy we're asking you to honor rather than a technical wall stopping a 6th device.

What if it's not the right fit for my business?

Try it free for 14 days first — no charge until the trial ends, cancel anytime before then with zero cost. If you subscribe and change your mind after that, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Checkout runs through Stripe and PayPal — the two most widely used payment processors in the world, supporting cards, digital wallets, and local payment methods across 100+ countries. We never see or store your card details.

The core tools are MIT-licensed — why do I need a serial code at all?

Good question, and we'd rather be upfront: the underlying code is open-source, so nothing here is unbreakable copy-protection. Your purchase pays for the packaged, ready-to-run build, ongoing updates and security fixes, priority support, and a simple way to track your own devices — not an exclusive license to code you couldn't otherwise read. We're trusting our customers to license fairly in exchange for keeping this affordable for everyone.
