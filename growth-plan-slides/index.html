The honest split

Small business vs. large business

🟢 Small business — your Day 30 buyer

Solo operators and 2–10 person teams buy fast, on their own credit card, with no procurement process — often same-day. 100% of your Day 30 revenue comes from here. Every tactic in this plan is built for this buyer.

🟡 Large business — a Month 2–3 seed, not a Day 30 close

Enterprise buyers need procurement, security review, references, a sales call — none of which compresses into 30 days for a product with zero paying customers yet. We seed this pipeline (OliFlow + Oli-Locator only) with LinkedIn outreach in Week 3, aiming for Month 2 discovery calls — not a Month 1 sale.

Any marketing director who promises an enterprise close inside 30 days for an unreferenced product is selling you a fantasy. This plan won't do that.

Section 4

Channel-by-channel: why, and the honest limit

🚀 Product Hunt

Biggest free traffic spike available. Limit: a one-day spike, not sustained traffic — value is in signups/social proof.

✉️ Cold outreach

Real estate agents, social managers, sellers respond to direct pitches, not directories. Limit: 5–10% reply rate is realistic — a volume game.

💬 Reddit / Indie Hackers

Free, and your buyers already discuss this exact pain there. Limit: punishes anything that reads as an ad — lead with a real story.

🔗 Directory listings

Free, compounding SEO + buyer-intent traffic. Limit: slow burn, builds over months not days.

📧 Email nurture

Highest-converting channel — these people already raised their hand. Limit: only as good as the traffic feeding it.

🏢 Enterprise LinkedIn seeding

OliFlow + Oli-Locator have genuine enterprise fit. Limit: a Month 2–3 pipeline build, not a Day 30 revenue line.
